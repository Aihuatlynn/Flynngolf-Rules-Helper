## Full description (max 4000)
Golf Rules Helper is an independent study aid for golfers, volunteers and rules enthusiasts who want to learn how the Rules of Golf apply to everyday situations - with no sign-up and no internet connection.

Describe a situation, type a few keywords or paste a rules question, and the app picks the matching Rules situation and shows:
- the likely ruling and the Rule number to look up
- the penalty (no penalty, one stroke, general penalty, disqualification)
- the stroke arithmetic, step by step

FOUR WAYS TO PRACTISE
- Section A - True or False
- Section B - Multiple choice: paste the options and see which one fits best, with reasons
- Section C - Identify the penalty and the Rule number
- Section D - Identify the Rule, then calculate the strokes incurred

FEATURES
- 44 ready-made sample questions to try with one tap
- Plain-language definitions of key golf terms (e.g. general area, penalty area, abnormal course condition, unplayable ball)
- Relief and penalty summaries for common situations: ball moved, wrong ball, out of bounds, lost ball, penalty areas, bunkers, putting green, practice and advice
- "Check the official wording" links to the Rules of Golf published by The R&A (opens in your browser)
- 100% offline after install. No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected

IMPORTANT
Golf Rules Helper is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or approved by The R&A, the USGA or any golf association. "Rules of Golf" is the work of The R&A and the USGA. The app gives study guidance based on a simplified understanding of the 2023 Rules; it can be wrong or incomplete. For competitions and official decisions always check the current official Rules of Golf and Decisions, or ask a qualified referee.
