A study aid that paraphrases the 2023 Rules of Golf (The R&A). It is not the official text and can be wrong, especially for unusual cases and Local Rules. Check the linked rule page and the Official Guide before relying on an answer, This app is for learning and practice only. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The R&A, the USGA or any golf association, and it should not be used during a competition or any official assessment.
